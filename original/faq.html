Frequently asked questions

The details before the details.

Answers to common questions about starting a custom glassware project.

Planning your project.

Which products can I discuss?

Drinkware, tableware, barware, home decor, and coffee and tea glassware. The Products page lists representative catalog models to help you describe a starting point.

Can I request a new shape or capacity?

Yes. Include your target dimensions, capacity, and a sketch or reference. Feasibility, tooling requirements, and commercial terms need to be reviewed before a custom design is confirmed.

Can glass color, decoration, and finish be combined?

You can include all three in your brief. Suitable combinations depend on the selected product, the desired appearance, and the intended use. They are confirmed during project review.

What artwork should I prepare?

Send a vector file if available, along with placement, approximate size, and color references. If you only have a concept image, use it to explain the intended result.

Is custom packaging available?

Packaging can be part of the project discussion. Include the number of pieces per pack, box style, protection requirements, and any retail or gifting needs.

What is the minimum order quantity?

There is no single published minimum across all projects. Share your estimated quantity; the applicable requirement will depend on the product and customization scope.

How long will a project take?

Timing depends on the product, development needs, decoration, packaging, and order details. Include your target date so the schedule can be reviewed before any commitment is made.

Can I request samples?

Include your sample needs in the inquiry. Availability, charges, and timing must be confirmed for the selected product and project.

Are the glasses dishwasher-safe or suitable for hot drinks?

Do not assume suitability from the product image or category alone. Ask for confirmation for the exact material, decoration, and intended use before selecting a product.

Do the images show exact catalog models?

The images on this website are AI-generated concepts used to illustrate product families and design directions. They are not exact photographs of the listed models. Confirm appearance and specifications through product review and, where agreed, samples.

Do you provide certifications or test reports?

Tell us which market and requirements apply to your project. Relevant documentation and testing needs must be reviewed for the specific product; no blanket certification is implied.

What should I include in an inquiry?

Your product category, reference model if known, customization priorities, estimated quantity, and target timing. Add artwork or packaging references when available.
