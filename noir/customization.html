Customization

Your brand, in every detail.

Build a coherent product direction across the vessel, decoration, finish, and packaging. Start with the choices that matter to your project.

Five areas to shape your brief.

AI-generated concept image. Final product appearance is confirmed during project review.

01 / Shape & Capacity

Start with the way it will be used.

Choose a catalog profile or describe a new shape direction. Share your target capacity, overall dimensions, and any fit requirements.

What to share

Reference model or sketch; target capacity; height and diameter; intended use.

02 / Glass Color

Set the tone of the collection.

Define a clear, tinted, or colored direction. Include a color reference and explain whether the desired effect should be transparent, translucent, or opaque. The suitable approach depends on the product.

What to share

Color reference; transparency preference; areas to be colored; matching priorities.

03 / Logo & Decoration

Give the piece a recognizable identity.

Share the artwork, preferred placement, and approximate decoration size. Describe the visual effect you want; the suitable decoration method will need to be reviewed for the chosen glassware.

What to share

Vector artwork if available; placement; dimensions; number of colors; reference images.

04 / Surface Finish

Refine the look and feel.

Describe the finish you have in mind, such as a clear or frosted appearance. Identify whether it should cover the whole piece or selected areas, and explain the expected use and cleaning conditions.

What to share

Finish reference; coverage area; intended handling; cleaning requirements.

05 / Custom Packaging

Complete the presentation.

Plan around the number of pieces, protection needs, and the unboxing experience. Share your preferred box style, artwork, and whether the project is intended for retail, gifting, or another setting.

What to share

Pieces per pack; box reference; artwork; inserts; labeling and shipping needs.

Confirm the combination before moving forward.

Color, decoration, finish, and packaging choices should be reviewed together. Product feasibility, sample requirements, costs, and timing are project-specific and must be confirmed before an order.
